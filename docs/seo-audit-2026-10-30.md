**Checked and fine on every page:** HTTPS, canonical tags, a single main headline (H1), meta descriptions of 100 to 160 characters, clean URLs, mobile-friendly layout, valid structured data (business, plans, FAQ, articles, breadcrumbs), sitemap entries, internal links (11 to 17 per page).

**Flagged by the crawler but not actually problems:**
- *"Missing image alt":* these are decorative images (the hero mockup, example screenshots, the author photo next to the author's name). Empty alt text is correct for them, which is why accessibility scores 100.
- *"FAQ schema missing answerCount":* that field is only required for Q&A forum pages, not FAQ sections. Google accepts your FAQ markup as is. Note that Google now shows FAQ rich results mostly for government and health sites, but the markup still helps search engines and AI answers understand the page.
- *"Misspellings":* brand and technical words like Ryzon, HVAC, SEO, Zapier and Squarespace.
- *"Render-blocking resources":* the site's stylesheet. Speed scores are 93 to 99, so there's nothing worth changing.

**One small speed note:** Google Tag Manager and Analytics add about 0.2 to 0.3 seconds of processing on phones (the home page's "total blocking time" is 303ms). It's acceptable. If you ever add more tracking scripts, watch this number.
