# Keyword research, October 2026

Source: DataForSEO (Labs keyword ideas, keyword suggestions, keyword overview, ranked keywords; live Google SERPs with "People also ask"). Market: United States, English. Volumes are average monthly Google searches. KD is keyword difficulty, 0–100 (lower is easier for a new site).

## What the data says

- **Generic "website builder" terms are out of reach.** "small business website builder" and its variants get thousands of searches, but KD is 51–100 and Wix, Squarespace, GoDaddy and review sites own the results.
- **Cost questions are big and easy.** "how much does it cost to build a website" gets 2,400/mo at KD 14. Maintenance and hosting costs are KD 5–19.
- **Trade-specific design terms are easy and commercial.** "contractor website design" is 1,000/mo at KD 12. Plumber, HVAC, roofing and electrician variants are KD 3–15.
- **Local SEO questions are a good fit for the offer.** "local seo for small business" is 1,300/mo at KD 21. The ranking pages are generic (Reddit, LinkedIn, US Chamber) and none is written plainly for owners.
- **Local "web design + city" terms are small but nearly uncontested.** "web design detroit" is 320/mo at KD 4. These suit location pages more than blog posts.

### Content gaps they fill

- **Local SEO:** top results are a Reddit thread, LinkedIn posts and broad explainers. Ours adds a 30-day checklist, Google Business Profile field-by-field, review rules and an AI-search section (PAA: "Is SEO dead now with AI?").
- **Contractor websites:** top results are example galleries (Dribbble, Webflow, Colorlib) and agency homepages. Ours is the practical "what pages and trust signals do I need" guide, split by trade.
- **Maintenance cost:** top results quote "$5 to $25,000 a month". Ours is scoped to a small business site, itemized, with signs of overpaying and what to ask before signing.
