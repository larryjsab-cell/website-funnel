## Terms (confirmed)
- **Ownership:** Ryzon owns and manages the site for year one. After that, a customer who leaves gets their site files exported and delivered to them.
- **Demo turnaround:** as soon as the next day.
- **Cancel:** anytime. Cancel before year one and the design files are not included.
- **Edits:** Complete includes free changes for life. On Starter and Growth, edits are per project: simple tasks may be free depending on tenure, otherwise $20/hour.
- **Demo changes:** once the demo is sent, changes start with a plan, unless the client wants a completely different design to decide.
